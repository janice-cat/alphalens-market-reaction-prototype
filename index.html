About AlphaLens

Prototype framing

AlphaLens is an experimental market-reaction simulator. It does not try to predict exact prices. Instead, it classifies global events into economic transmission channels and generates plausible z-score response curves across semiconductors, broad tech, utilities, and energy.

The current prototype uses local seed data, rule-based event classification, parameterized response templates, and lightweight explanation logic. The intended production version would replace the rule-based layer with stronger model-based classification and calibrate templates on a larger verified event set.

This is a research prototype and not financial advice. Outputs are simulated scenarios, not trading recommendations.
