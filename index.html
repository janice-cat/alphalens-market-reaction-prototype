Method

Two methods share same event understanding, but answer timing differently.

Empirical Templates

Blends calibrated response templates learned from similar events.

Lead-Lag

Preserves the order reactions unfold across assets by blending horizon-level response paths.

Both modes share the same event understanding layer; they differ only in how the final path is constructed.

  • Measured event-window calibration is the next major upgrade path.

Why It Matters

AlphaLens turns market news into a structured, testable reaction map.

Instead of leaving market narratives vague, it makes the assumptions explicit: what the event is, which channels matter, who moves first, and why the move happens.
